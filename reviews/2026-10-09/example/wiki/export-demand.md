# Export demand

These findings concern synthetic evaluation fixtures only.

## What the evidence supports

- **Sourced claim:** Participant A requests CSV for moving report results into a planning spreadsheet and says they would use it weekly. [Interview A](../raw/customer-interviews.md#interview-a)
- **Sourced claim:** Participant B prefers scheduled Monday email to logging in and downloading a file. [Interview B](../raw/customer-interviews.md#interview-b) The research contains two convenience-sample interviews and no segment breakdown. [Research method](../raw/customer-interviews.md#report-delivery-interviews)
- **Sourced claim:** During September 2026 (UTC), the existing account-list CSV feature recorded 120 successful export events from 18 distinct exporting users. Events count repeated exports separately; these are not saved-report exports. [Usage definitions and observations](../raw/usage-export-note.md#definitions-and-observations)
- **Derived:** There is evidence of a CSV workflow for one interviewed participant and activity on a different existing export feature. Neither establishes how widespread saved-report CSV demand is. The second interview suggests delivery convenience may matter for some users. [Interviews](../raw/customer-interviews.md) [Usage scope](../raw/usage-export-note.md#definitions-and-observations)

## What remains assumed

- **Uncertain:** Saved-report CSV export will improve retention. The proposal explicitly describes this as a hypothesis; the usage note includes no retention comparison. [Product hypothesis](../raw/product-export-proposal.md#problem-and-proposal) [Usage limits](../raw/usage-export-note.md#limits)
- **Open question:** How many customers want CSV versus scheduled delivery, which workflows and segments they represent, and whether they would actually adopt either option. Two stated preferences cannot establish prevalence. [Interview method](../raw/customer-interviews.md)
- **Open question:** Export adoption rate and retention effect cannot be calculated from this note because it lacks an active-user denominator and retention outcomes. [Usage limits](../raw/usage-export-note.md#limits)

## What to validate next

**Derived recommendations:**

1. Interview additional users in the relevant segments about their report reuse and delivery workflows. Test CSV and scheduled email prototypes against those workflows.
2. Define success before implementation: relevant eligible users, successful saved-report exports, repeated use, and an explicit retention measure and observation window. Distinguish account-list and saved-report events.
3. If retention is the intended outcome, run an appropriately designed experiment with comparable eligible groups. Export event counts alone cannot establish a causal retention benefit.

These recommendations respond to the [small interview sample](../raw/customer-interviews.md), [undefined product success measure](../raw/product-export-proposal.md#proposed-behavior), and [missing usage comparisons](../raw/usage-export-note.md#limits).
