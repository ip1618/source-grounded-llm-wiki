# Wiki schema

- Pages: topic pages plus this index and append-only log. Raw files are original evidence records and must remain unchanged.
- Status: **Sourced claim** for attributed evidence; **Derived** for synthesis; **Uncertain** for unsupported hypotheses; **Open question** for missing evidence.
- Citations: link to `../raw/filename.md` with a supporting section anchor or line locator. Cite near each meaningful sourced claim.
- Links: relative Markdown links. Each durable topic belongs in the index.
- Updates: search the index and topic pages first; edit only relevant pages; append dated log entries. Queries are read-only unless saving is requested.
- Sources are synthetic test fixtures; do not generalize their findings to real customers.
