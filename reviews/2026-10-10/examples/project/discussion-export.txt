For money I recommend storing cents as integers. With binary floating-point, ordinary decimal prices can produce small representation differences, which makes totals and reconciliation harder to explain. Cents also match the precision of the amounts we currently need. Tax can stay in basis points, where 100 basis points is 1 percent, and we can round the tax amount once to the nearest cent, with halves rounded up.
