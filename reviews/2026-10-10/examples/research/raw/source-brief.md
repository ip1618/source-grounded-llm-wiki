# Queue review field note

Synthetic research source. Fictional experiment; no customer or personal data.
Author: Rowan, research analyst
Date: 2026-07-03
Status: initial internal field note

We observed 240 invoice-preparation sessions during a small workflow trial. A session began when an operator created a draft and ended when the draft first became publicly accessible. Sessions that never reached that event remained in the completion-rate denominator.

The initial export reported 99 completed rows from the 240-session cohort, summarized here as a 41% completion rate. The same 41% figure appears in the planning deck because it copied this note. We have not independently reconciled completed rows to unique sessions yet.

The median publish delay among completed sessions was 64 minutes. In this note, publish delay runs from draft_created to first_public_access; it includes authoring and waiting for approval. That value is not a service response-time measure.

Operators often paused to clarify a service description before final approval. The observed pattern suggests wording review could be useful, but this observation does not establish that unclear descriptions caused every delay.
