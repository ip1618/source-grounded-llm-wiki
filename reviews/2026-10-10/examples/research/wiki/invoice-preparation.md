# Invoice preparation trial

This page owns the trial's completion measure, publish-delay definitions, and interpretation. The experiment is fictional. The current completion count comes from the later amendment; the supplied sources have not been independently verified. [Source register](index.md#source-register).

## Study scope

**Sourced claim:** The small workflow trial observed 240 invoice-preparation sessions. A session began at draft creation and ended at first public access. Sessions that did not reach public access stayed in the completion-rate denominator. The amendment retains the same cohort. [Field note](../raw/source-brief.md), line 8; [amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), lines 8–10.

## Completion

**Sourced claim — current:** Reconciliation gives 91 unique completed sessions out of 240: 37.9%, rounded to 38%. Eight repeated completion rows from retried exports explain the earlier numerator of 99. [Amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), line 8.

**Derived:** The count was corrected within the same cohort, so these notes do not establish a performance change. No comparable later cohort was supplied. [Amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), lines 8–10.

## Possible causes and next evidence

**Sourced observation:** Operators often paused to clarify a service description before final approval; the amendment repeats that observation. **Uncertain:** wording's contribution to completion or delay remains unestablished. The small observational sample supplies no wording intervention or measured outcome from one. [Field note](../raw/source-brief.md), line 14; [amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), line 14.

**Proposed next step:** review service wording in a future trial. No such trial is approved or reported. [Amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), line 14.

**Open questions:** Does a later comparable cohort improve completion and publish delay using the same timing definitions? Which factors explain the pauses? [Amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), lines 8, 12, and 14.

## Meaningful history

- **Completion discrepancy — Resolved; earlier claim Superseded:** The initial field note reported 99 completed rows and 41%, with reconciliation pending. The planning deck copied that result. The amendment directly identifies eight repeated rows and replaces the count with 91 unique sessions/38%; the deck's copied 41% is also superseded as a reconciled rate. Copied or repeated statements are not independent corroboration. [Field note](../raw/source-brief.md), lines 10 and 16; [amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), lines 8–10.
- **Publish-delay difference — Explained:** The 64-minute and 29-minute values measure different intervals. Both remain useful with their definitions; the narrower interval does not replace the end-to-end measure. No open numerical conflict remains after comparing definitions. [Field note](../raw/source-brief.md), line 12; [amendment](../raw/source-amendment.md), line 12.
